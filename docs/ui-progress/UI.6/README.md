# UI.6 — Apparatus and handling

## UI.6.1 — Stands, clamps and holders

`titration.png` shows a titration set-up assembled from the tray by dragging
alone:

- a retort stand, with a white tile on its base;
- a 250 mL conical flask standing on the tile;
- a 50 mL burette gripped by the clamp, its tip in the flask's neck. It holds
  10 mL of sodium hydroxide, read at the 40 mark from 0 at the top.

`holder-gauze.png` shows two Bunsen burners with gauze on their tripods:

- a test tube over one, gripped by a wooden test-tube holder;
- a small beaker over the other, standing on the gauze.

## UI.6.2 — Pouring that looks like pouring

- **`pour.png`:** a small beaker of blue copper sulfate solution, mid-pour into
  an empty one. It is tipped 65° at its lip over the mouth of the other, and
  its liquid surface stays level. The stream arcs from the lip down onto the
  floor of the empty beaker, which is filling from the bottom.
- **`pour-high.png`:** the same pour, let go well above the beaker. A long
  stream falls into it and splashes where it lands. The same 10 mL was poured.

## UI.6.3 — Dropwise, swirling and stirring

- **`dropper.png`:** a dropping pipette over a small beaker of sodium
  hydroxide in Dropper mode. Its red bulb has just been squeezed, and a single
  drop is falling toward the liquid.
- **`stir-swirl.png`:** the titration set-up after a single drop from the
  burette, with a glass rod stirring the small beaker.
