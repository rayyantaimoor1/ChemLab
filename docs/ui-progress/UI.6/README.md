# UI.6 — Apparatus and handling

## UI.6.1 — Stands, clamps and holders

`titration.png` shows a titration set-up assembled from the tray by dragging
alone:

- a retort stand, with a white tile on its base;
- a 250 mL conical flask standing on the tile;
- a 50 mL burette gripped by the clamp, its tip in the flask's neck. It holds
  10 mL of sodium hydroxide, read at the 40 mark from 0 at the top.

`holder-gauze.png` shows two Bunsen burners with gauze on their tripods:

- a test tube over one, gripped by a wooden test-tube holder;
- a small beaker over the other, standing on the gauze.
