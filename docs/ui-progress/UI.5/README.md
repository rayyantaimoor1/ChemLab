# UI.5 — Gas you can see

## UI.5.2 — Bubbles where the gas forms

`bubbles.png` shows four small beakers on the bench. The bubbles are frozen
mid-rise and the hydrogen hazard alert has been dismissed.

- **Magnesium, zinc and iron, each just covered with 1 M hydrochloric acid:**
  - magnesium has 16 bubbles rising off the ribbon (vigorous);
  - zinc has 9 off the granules (steady);
  - iron has 4 off the filings (slow), and its solution has turned pale green.
- **Sodium chloride solution with the current on:** bubbles stream off both
  rods, equally - hydrogen at the cathode (−) and chlorine at the anode (+).
  The rods dip down into the liquid.

## UI.5.3 — Coloured gases, fumes and smoke

`gases.png` shows four small beakers. The hazard alerts have been dismissed.

- **Sodium nitrite with dilute HCl:** reddish brown nitrogen dioxide gathered
  above the liquid. The label names it.
- **Sodium chloride solution with the current on:** pale yellow-green chlorine
  above the liquid, with the electrodes in it.
- **Salt with concentrated sulfuric acid:** a puff of steamy fumes leaving the
  mouth.
- **Ammonia meeting hydrogen chloride:** dense white ammonium chloride smoke
  filling the beaker, where the two gases met.

`fumes.png` shows two small beakers:

- **Concentrated hydrochloric acid:** a white mist rising from the mouth. The
  label says "white fumes from concentrated hydrochloric acid".
- **Bromine:** a red-brown wisp of its own vapour at the mouth. The label says
  "dark red-brown bromine vapour at the mouth".
