# UI.8 — The room

## UI.8.1 — Bench and wall

`bench-and-wall.png` is the whole window at 1366×768:

- the titration set-up stands on the dark resin bench top;
- dark slate-green glazed wall tiles rise behind it;
- the light falls from the left.

The two textures are drawn by `scripts/make-room-images.mjs`:
`wall-tiles.jpg` is 2,650 bytes and `bench-resin.jpg` 1,523 bytes.

## UI.8.2 — Real reagent bottles

`bottles.png` shows the shelf filtered to "nitr":

- **On the shelf:**
  - nitric acid and potassium nitrate in clear reagent bottles;
  - nitrogen in a gas cylinder;
  - ammonium nitrate in a screw-cap jar;
  - silver nitrate in amber glass.
- **Silver nitrate's properties card:** its amber bottle, with a label
  printed with its name, AgNO3, 0.1 M, and the oxidising, corrosive and
  environment pictograms.

## UI.8.3 — Fume cupboard

`fume-cupboard.png` shows a small beaker of bleach and dilute hydrochloric
acid standing inside the fume cupboard, on its worktop behind the sash:

- **The gas:** its chlorine is thin and pale yellow-green, and yellow-green
  wisps rise toward the duct.
- **The label:** "pale yellow-green chlorine gas above the liquid · in the fume
  cupboard".
- **The airflow light** is green.
- **The hazard alert** (dismissed before the screenshot) appeared in full.

## UI.8.4 — The notebook as paper

`full-screen.png` is the whole window at 1366×768:

- **Left:** the shelf of reagent bottles.
- **Middle:** a titration set-up - a burette clamped over a conical flask of
  blue copper sulfate solution on a white tile - on the resin bench against the
  tiled wall.
- **Right:** the notebook as 5 mm grid paper. The app's entries are in print,
  and the student's observation, "Clear blue solution in the flask, burette
  ready", is in their own hand in blue-black pen.

This is also the screenshot for UI.8's check. It still has to be shown to
someone seeing the app for the first time.
