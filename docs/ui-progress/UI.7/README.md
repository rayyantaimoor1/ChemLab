## UI.7.1 — Look closely

`close-look.png` shows a small beaker of copper sulfate solution with sodium
hydroxide, opened in the close look at 2.4× on a white tile:

- **The glass:** grey walls and dark printed graduations, as glass looks
  against white.
- **The liquid:** pale blue, with its meniscus on the 20 mL line.
- **The details beneath:** "Liquid: pale blue", "Solid formed: pale blue

## UI.7.2 — Reading at eye level

`reading-above.png` shows a 50 mL burette holding 10 mL in the close look,
with "Eye above" chosen and the panel scrolled down to the reading lens:

- **The lens:** 0.1 mL marks with 39, 40 and 41 labelled, shifted against
  the curved meniscus, which sits at about 39.7.
- **The reading:** "You read 39.70 mL run out - at eye level it is
  40.00 mL".
- **The explanation:** from above, a burette reads too low; read with your
  eye level with the bottom of the meniscus.
